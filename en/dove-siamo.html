Arriving by Car

A14 Bologna-Ancona Highway, exit at Rimini Sud. Proceed along the SS72 Rimini-San Marino expressway. Once in the Republic, follow signs for "Centro Storico" up to public parking lots P6 and P7.

Practical Information

Parking, ZTL & Transport

Parking & ZTL

Direct vehicle arrival at the hotel front entrance is not possible as it is located in a pedestrian Limited Traffic Zone (ZTL). Convenient barrier-operated public parking lots P6 and P7 are just a 2-3 minute walk away.

Collect your ticket at the entrance and park in the designated blue spaces (spots cannot be reserved in advance). Public parking rate: €6 per day, valid until 23:59 on your check-out day. Full assistance and directions are provided upon arrival.

Arriving by Bus

Daily scheduled bus connections between Rimini Train Station and San Marino (both weekdays and holidays). Tickets can be easily purchased on board before departure.

Official routes and timetables: benedettinispa.com and bonellibus.it.
