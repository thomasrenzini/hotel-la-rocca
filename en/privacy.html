Privacy Policy & Cookies

Personal Data Processing Policy in accordance with San Marino Law No. 171/2018 and EU Regulation 2016/679 (GDPR).

1. Data Controller

The Data Controller is LA ROCCA SRL (sole shareholder company) – hospitality structure Hotel La Rocca, with registered and operational headquarters at Via Salita alla Rocca n° 33/35 – 47890 City of San Marino (Republic of San Marino), C.O.E. SM 30254. For any privacy requests or to exercise data subject rights, you may contact the Controller at:

  • Email: hotellarocca@omniway.sm
  • Phone: +378 0549 991166

2. Data Collection Methods, Hosting, and Absence of Online Database Storage

This website serves purely informative purposes. Personal data voluntarily submitted by users through quote request or contact forms are neither saved nor stored on any website database. Data is directly and solely transmitted in real time via email protocol to the property's official mailbox (managed through email applications such as Microsoft Outlook and Google Gmail and received at hotellarocca@omniway.sm) for the exclusive purpose of processing and replying to the inquiry or quotation requested.

Web hosting, server, and network infrastructure services are provided by Aruba S.p.A. (with data centers and servers located within the European Union), appointed as technical supplier and data processor for the technical management of web space, alongside authorized email service providers.

3. Categories of Processed Data & Protection of Special Category Data

  • Identification and Contact Details: full name, email address, telephone number.
  • Stay and Booking Inquiry Details: arrival and departure dates, room type selected, number of guests (adults/children), and general notes. Guests are kindly requested not to provide health-related, sensitive, or special category data (Art. 9 GDPR) regarding medical conditions or allergies unless strictly necessary for managing accommodation logistics.
  • Browsing Data & Cookies: IP addresses and technical device parameters. The website uses technical cookies only (such as language preference and cookie consent settings) which do not require prior consent. Only upon explicit and optional user consent via the Cookie Banner, the 'Location' page loads the interactive map service Google Maps (provided by Google Ireland Limited). Enabling Google Maps may involve the installation of third-party cookies and data transfer to third countries (including the United States of America), governed by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Purposes and Legal Basis of Processing

Personal data is processed lawfully pursuant to Art. 5 of San Marino Law No. 171/2018 and Art. 6 of the EU GDPR for:

  • Performance of Pre-contractual and Contractual Measures: answering availability and quotation requests, managing room reservations, and arranging requested activities/excursions.
  • Compliance with Legal and Public Security Obligations: mandatory notification of guest arrivals to the Public Security Authorities of the Republic of San Marino, as well as fulfillment of accounting, tax, and administrative duties.

5. Processing Methods and Retention Periods

Processing is carried out using electronic and paper tools with strict organizational and technical security measures.

  • Unconfirmed quote requests: emails are retained solely for the period required to handle the inquiry and periodically deleted within a maximum of 12 months.
  • Confirmed stays: accounting, billing, and administrative records are retained for the statutory 10-year period prescribed by San Marino law.

6. Disclosure to Third Parties and Recipients

Data will never be disseminated, transferred, or sold to third parties for commercial or marketing purposes. Data may only be disclosed to: authorized internal personnel, San Marino Public Security Authorities, banking institutions, Aruba S.p.A. (for web hosting infrastructure), email providers, company tax/legal consultants, and local tourism operators only upon explicit guest request for outdoor excursion bookings.

7. Rights of the Data Subject

Data subjects may at any time exercise their statutory rights under Articles 15-22 of San Marino Law No. 171/2018 and the GDPR (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection) by writing to hotellarocca@omniway.sm, or lodge a formal complaint with the Data Protection Authority of the Republic of San Marino (www.garanteprivacy.sm).

8. Automated Decision-Making and Profiling

The Data Controller does not use automated decision-making processes or profiling activities on user data.

Privacy Assistance: For any inquiries regarding our privacy policy or data processing, the front desk of Hotel La Rocca is at your full disposal via email at hotellarocca@omniway.sm or by phone at +378 0549 991166.
