2. Quotation Requests and Booking Finalization

Submitting a request on this website constitutes a non-binding availability inquiry and does not create an automatic reservation. A booking is finalized and binding only upon receipt of written confirmation sent by Hotel La Rocca front desk via email, following receipt of any agreed deposit or credit card guarantee.

3. Rates, All Inclusive Minibar and Breakfast

  • Included Services: Room rates include accommodation, high-speed Wi-Fi, individually controlled air conditioning/heating, ozone environmental sanitization, and All Inclusive In-Room Minibar.
  • Breakfast and Bar (Excluded): Breakfast is not included in the standard room rate. Guests are welcome to enjoy the internal hotel bar for morning breakfast, snacks, and aperitifs as an optional paid service.

4. Check-in and Check-out Times

  • Check-in: from 2:00 PM to 7:00 PM. Advance notice is mandatory for arrivals expected after 7:00 PM.
  • Check-out: strictly by 10:00 AM on the day of departure.
  • Upon check-in, all guests (including minors) must present a valid government-issued photo ID for statutory registration with the San Marino public security authorities.

5. Cancellation and No-Show Policies

Unless different conditions are specified in writing in the booking confirmation:

  • Free Cancellation: up to 3 days prior to the scheduled arrival date, no penalty applies.
  • Late Cancellation or No-Show: for cancellations made less than 3 days before arrival or in case of no-show, the hotel will retain the deposit or charge the first night of the stay.

6. Exclusion of the 14-Day Right of Withdrawal

As this contract concerns the provision of accommodation services for non-residential purposes with a specific date or period of performance, pursuant to Art. 16(l) of European Directive 2011/83/EU, Art. 59 of the Consumer Code, and applicable consumer protection laws of the Republic of San Marino, the standard 14-day right of withdrawal for distance contracts does not apply. Only the cancellation policies set out in Section 5 shall apply.

7. ZTL Restricted Traffic Zone Access and Public Parking (Non-affiliated)

Hotel La Rocca is situated within the pedestrian area of the Historic Centre of San Marino, inside a Restricted Traffic Zone (ZTL).

  • The hotel cannot be reached by car. The hotel disclaims any liability for fines incurred due to unauthorized vehicle entry into the ZTL.
  • Public barrier car parks no. 6 and no. 7 are located approximately 3 minutes' walk away (blue spaces, non-reservable). The car park is not affiliated with the hotel and charges a public rate of €6.00 per day (valid until 11:59 PM on the check-out day).

8. Excursions and Outdoor Activities Booking

All guided experiences (trekking, e-bike tours, cultural visits, wine tastings, archery, flying experiences) must be requested at least 3 days in advance of your arrival date by contacting the hotel reception.

9. Smoking Policy and Pets

  • Smoking is strictly prohibited in all bedrooms and indoor spaces of the hotel.
  • Small pets are permitted exclusively upon prior request and written authorization confirmed by management during the booking process.

For any inquiries, remarks, or complaints during or after your stay, guests are encouraged to contact our front desk promptly via email at hotellarocca@omniway.sm or by phone at +378 0549 991166 to enable an amicable and immediate resolution.

The accommodation contract is governed exclusively by the laws of the Republic of San Marino. Any dispute that cannot be resolved amicably shall fall under the exclusive jurisdiction of the Single Court of the Republic of San Marino (Tribunale Unico della Repubblica di San Marino).

Front Desk Support: Our team is at your complete disposal for any clarification via phone at +378 0549 991166 or email at hotellarocca@omniway.sm.
