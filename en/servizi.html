Our Services

Complete Hospitality

From check-in to check-out, we take care of every aspect of your stay.

Hotel Bar

Our welcoming bar is located right inside the hotel for breakfast, aperitifs, and relaxing moments. Bar drinks and snacks are not included in the room rate (optional paid service).

Free Wi-Fi

High-speed Internet connection available for free in all rooms and common areas of the hotel. Stay connected during your stay.

Nearby Parking

Detailed information on public and affiliated parking areas (e.g. Parking 6 and 7) just 2-3 minutes away on foot. We will guide you to reach the hotel comfortably.

Luggage Storage

Free luggage storage service before check-in and after check-out, allowing you to enjoy San Marino completely worry-free.

Personalized Reception

Our staff is always at your disposal for tips on restaurants, attractions, scenic walking paths, and everything that makes your San Marino stay unforgettable.

Panoramic Views

Rooms with breathtaking vistas overlooking the rolling hills, the scenic valleys of San Marino, and the historic fortress walls of Mount Titano.

Daily Housekeeping

Air Conditioning

In-Room TV

Useful Information

Access to the Historic Center & Parking

The hotel is located in the pedestrian historic center (ZTL limited traffic zone) not directly accessible by car. However, convenient public and affiliated parking facilities (e.g. Parking 6 and Parking 7) are available just a 2-3 minute walk away.

For luggage drop-off or any arrival assistance, our team is always on hand to advise you on the easiest walking route and ensure a smooth arrival.
