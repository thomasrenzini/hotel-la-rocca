Our Heritage

A Haven of Elegance in the Historic Center

Located in the very heart of the Republic of San Marino, Hotel La Rocca is the ideal starting point to explore this UNESCO World Heritage town.

Where History Meets Comfort

At Hotel La Rocca, located in the historic heart of San Marino, we offer a unique and unforgettable experience. Our family-run management is committed to providing a warm and welcoming atmosphere, with personalized service that will make you feel right at home. Thanks to our privileged location atop Mount Titano, you will enjoy breathtaking views of the historic town and the surrounding scenery.

Our rooms, equipped with all modern comforts, are designed to ensure maximum relaxation. We offer air conditioning, private bathrooms with hairdryer and shower enclosure, free Wi-Fi, an All Inclusive minibar, and plasma TV. We care about the environment and sustainability: room sanitization is carried out using ozone systems. Our staff is always at your disposal for tips, concierge assistance, and organizing your activities. Contact us directly by email or phone to request information and room photos.
